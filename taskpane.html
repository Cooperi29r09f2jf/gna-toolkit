    House rules

    1. Action titles. State the takeaway as a full sentence. Reading only the titles tells the story.
    2. One message per slide. If a slide needs two titles, it is two slides.
    3. Sentence case. Capitalize the first word and proper nouns only.
    4. No em dashes. Use a comma, colon or full stop.
    5. Lora only. Don't override the theme font.
    6. One teal highlight per slide. Teal leads, grey supports.
    7. Always cite. Source line bottom left: "Source: [Client] data" or "Source: GNA analysis".
    8. White content slides. Solid teal only on covers, statements and closing.
    9. No big-number layouts. Show numbers inline in compact rows.
