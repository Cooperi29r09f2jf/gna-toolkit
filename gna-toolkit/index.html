GNA Toolkit

The PowerPoint add-in Gheen & Noyes Advisory consultants use to build client decks from the GNA template.

Where to find it

Open PowerPoint signed in with your @gheennoyesadvisory.com account. The GNA Toolkit tab sits next to Home. If it isn't there yet, wait up to a day after it was deployed, then restart PowerPoint.

What's on the tab

  • Slide library: every template slide by category. Click to insert after the current slide.
  • Deck starters: Proposal, Findings, SteerCo and Workshop lineups, as a new deck or inserted into the open one.
  • Sticker, Source line, [NEEDED] flag: one-click house-style elements on the selected slides.
  • More tools: section tags, status dots, Harvey balls.
  • Style checker: finds em dashes, non-Lora fonts, leftover template text, missing sources and title problems, and fixes the safe ones.
